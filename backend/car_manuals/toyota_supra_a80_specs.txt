---
Vehicle: Toyota Supra A80 (1993)
Category: Fluids & Torque Specs
---

# Engine Oil
- 2JZ-GTE 3.0L Twin Turbo Engine
- Capacity: 5.2 Liters (with filter change)
- Recommended Viscosity: 5W-30 or 10W-30
- Drain Plug Torque: 39 Nm

# Transmission
- V160 6-Speed Manual (Getrag)
- Fluid Capacity: 1.8 Liters
- Recommended Fluid: Toyota V160 Fluid

# Brakes
- Front Caliper Bolts Torque: 118 Nm
- Rear Caliper Bolts Torque: 104 Nm
- Brake Fluid: DOT 3 or DOT 4

# Wheels
- Lug Nut Torque: 103 Nm
- Tire Pressure (Front): 36 PSI
- Tire Pressure (Rear): 36 PSI
