Category: Fluids & Torque Specs

# Engine Oil
- S14 2.3L Engine
- Capacity: 4.25 Liters (with filter change)
- Recommended Viscosity: 15W-50 or 20W-50
- Drain Plug Torque: 33 Nm

# Transmission
- Getrag 265 5-Speed Manual
- Fluid Capacity: 1.25 Liters
- Recommended Fluid: Red Line MTL or MT-90

# Brakes
- Front Caliper Bolts Torque: 110 Nm
- Rear Caliper Bolts Torque: 67 Nm
- Brake Fluid: DOT 4

# Wheels
- Lug Nut Torque: 110 Nm
- Tire Pressure (Front): 32 PSI
- Tire Pressure (Rear): 34 PSI
