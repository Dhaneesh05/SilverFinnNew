---
Vehicle: Audi RS7 Sportback
Category: Fluids & Torque Specs
---

# Engine Oil
- Capacity: 9.3 Liters (with filter change)
- Recommended Viscosity: 0W-40 or 5W-40 Fully Synthetic (VW 511.00 standard)
- Drain Plug Torque: 30 Nm

# Transmission
- ZF 8-Speed Automatic
- Fluid Capacity: 8.5 Liters (dry fill)
- Recommended Fluid: Audi G 060 162 A2

# Brakes
- Front Caliper Bolts Torque: 196 Nm
- Rear Caliper Bolts Torque: 35 Nm + 90 degrees
- Brake Fluid: DOT 4, replace every 2 years

# Wheels
- Lug Nut Torque: 120 Nm
- Tire Pressure (Front): 41 PSI (Unloaded)
- Tire Pressure (Rear): 38 PSI (Unloaded)
