---
Vehicle: Mercedes-Benz C63 S AMG (2019)
Category: Fluids & Torque Specs
---

# Engine Oil
- Capacity: 9.0 Liters (with filter change)
- Recommended Viscosity: 0W-40 or 5W-40 Fully Synthetic (MB 229.5 or MB 229.51)
- Drain Plug Torque: 30 Nm

# Transmission
- AMG SPEEDSHIFT MCT 9-Speed
- Fluid Capacity: 9.0 Liters
- Recommended Fluid: MB 236.15

# Brakes
- Front Caliper Bolts Torque: 115 Nm
- Rear Caliper Bolts Torque: 35 Nm + 45 degrees
- Brake Fluid: DOT 4 Plus, replace every 2 years

# Wheels
- Lug Nut Torque: 150 Nm
- Tire Pressure (Front): 36 PSI (Unloaded)
- Tire Pressure (Rear): 36 PSI (Unloaded)
