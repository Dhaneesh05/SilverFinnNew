---
Vehicle: Toyota Sprinter Trueno AE86 (1985)
Category: Fluids & Torque Specs
---

# Engine Oil
- 4A-GE 1.6L Engine
- Capacity: 3.3 Liters (with filter change)
- Recommended Viscosity: 10W-30 or 10W-40
- Drain Plug Torque: 25 Nm

# Transmission
- T50 5-Speed Manual
- Fluid Capacity: 1.7 Liters
- Recommended Fluid: GL-4 75W-90 Gear Oil

# Brakes
- Front Caliper Bolts Torque: 90 Nm
- Rear Caliper Bolts Torque: 45 Nm
- Brake Fluid: DOT 3

# Wheels
- Lug Nut Torque: 103 Nm
- Tire Pressure (Front): 28 PSI (Unloaded)
- Tire Pressure (Rear): 28 PSI (Unloaded)
